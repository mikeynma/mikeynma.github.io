Privacy policy: laptop-backup

laptop-backup is a personal backup tool used by one person, the owner of this site, to keep an encrypted copy of their own laptop in their own Google Drive. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

It asks Google only for access to files it creates itself (the drive.file permission). It cannot see, read or change any other file in the Drive.

What it does with data

Everything is encrypted on the laptop before it is uploaded, so the stored files can't be read without the owner's password. Nothing is sent anywhere but the owner's own Google Drive, nothing is shared with anyone, and no analytics or tracking are used.
